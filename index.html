About the estimates

Washington, D.C. and environs. 269 physical segments. Proposed passenger-use years include both endpoints and may describe different portions of a segment. They do not establish continuous service or construction/removal dates.

Confidence, uncertainty, mixed histories and citation cautions are available when you select a track. Map services and data available from U.S. Geological Survey, National Geospatial Program. This is a subdued modern basemap; background tiles load online and track data stays local.
