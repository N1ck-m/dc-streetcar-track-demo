READING THIS ATLAS

An envelope, with its uncertainty intact.

Dates can describe different portions of a generalized segment. Color reflects the weaker of the two endpoint estimates. It does not certify a route or uninterrupted operation.

141 segments have medium/high confidence at both ends; 128 have a low or very-low endpoint. Fourteen have explicit mixed-subextent records, and five need citation-scope review.

Method, boundaries & source files

Annual matching uses start_year ≤ selected year ≤ end_year. Endpoint ranges are inclusive editorial plausible bounds, not statistical confidence intervals. A zero-width range is still an estimate. All verified date fields are empty.

Known interruptions and alternative histories are retained in each evidence record without being silently converted into continuous intervals or year exclusions. The 14 mixed-subextent flags are not a complete inventory of possible mixed histories.

WGS 84 / EPSG:4326 longitude–latitude coordinates. The display uses a local equirectangular projection. No geography is invented or downloaded. Sources were not re-opened for this demo.
